# Research Brief: South Africa Major Cities Municipality Contact Information

## Objective
Collect all relevant phone numbers and email addresses for emergency services, municipal departments, and key contacts across South Africa's three major metropolitan regions:

### Region 1: KwaZulu-Natal
- **Durban (eThekwini Municipality)**
- **KwaDukuza Municipality** (formerly Stanger)
- **iLembe District Municipality**

### Region 2: Western Cape
- **Cape Town (City of Cape Town Metropolitan Municipality)**
- **Stellenbosch Municipality**
- **Drakenstein Municipality** (Paarl, Wellington)
- **George Municipality**
- **Overstrand Municipality** (Hermanus)

### Region 3: Gauteng (Johannesburg Area)
- **City of Johannesburg Metropolitan Municipality**
- **Ekurhuleni Metropolitan Municipality** (East Rand)
- **City of Tshwane Metropolitan Municipality** (Pretoria)
- **Midrand**
- **Sandton**

## Target Audience
This is for the CityWatcher app demo targeting residents and visitors in South Africa's three major metropolitan regions.

### 1. Emergency Services

### 2. Municipal Departments

## Information Format Required

For each contact, please provide:
```

Toll-Free Number: [0800 numbers if available]
Email Address(es): [Official emails]
SMS/WhatsApp: [If available for reporting]
Operating Hours: [If available]
Emergency/24-hour: [Yes/No]
Website: [If available]
Physical Address: [If relevant]
Languages Supported: [English, Afrikaans, Zulu, Xhosa, etc.]
```
